# ArticlePod Short-Form Launch Kit

This kit turns the May 2026 ArticlePod launch plan into production-ready short-form assets. It is intentionally content-only: no product, API, schema, or runtime behavior changes are required.

## Launch Positioning

Primary message:

> ArticlePod turns saved articles, text-based PDFs, and Chrome captures into a private knowledge feed: podcast episodes, summaries, snippets, digests, and a personal wiki.

Audience:

- Busy builders, engineers, founders, researchers, and knowledge workers.
- People who save more articles than they finish.
- People who already listen to podcasts and want learning to fit into daily routines.

Creator voice:

- First-person founder voice by default: "I built ArticlePod..."
- Use calm, practical, lived-experience language.
- Show product proof within the first 6-10 seconds.

## Platform Defaults

- Master video: `1080x1920`, 9:16 vertical, MP4/H.264, AAC audio.
- Target length: 25-45 seconds for most videos.
- Format: founder on camera for 3-6 seconds, then screen proof with voiceover.
- Captions: burned-in, high contrast, no more than two short lines at once.
- Safe zones: keep important text away from top, bottom, and right-side UI controls.
- Use one master export across LinkedIn, YouTube Shorts, X, Instagram Reels, TikTok, and Facebook Reels.

Source checks:

- LinkedIn native upload supports common video formats, up to 15 minutes, and warns to keep key elements out of edge safe zones: <https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a548372/video-sharing-troubleshooting-guide?lang=en>
- LinkedIn recommends hooks, vertical filming, on-screen context, and roughly 30 seconds to two minutes as a guide: <https://content.linkedin.com/content/dam/help/linkedin/en-us/LinkedIn-Sharing-Guide.pdf>
- YouTube classifies square or vertical videos up to three minutes as Shorts: <https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15424877?hl=en>
- X non-Premium upload supports videos up to 140 seconds and 512 MB: <https://help.x.com/en/using-x/x-videos>
- TikTok recommends 9:16 vertical for in-feed creative: <https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/video-ads-specifications?Tag=Page%2520Titles&redirected=1>

## Reusable Edit Template

1. Cold open, 0-3s: founder hook or visual problem.
2. Product proof, 3-18s: show the exact ArticlePod surface.
3. Outcome, 18-33s: show what changes for the user.
4. CTA, final 3-5s: ask for trial, comment, or feedback.

Recurring on-screen labels:

- "Your tabs, as podcasts"
- "One article. One private episode."
- "Save. Listen. Retain."
- "Private RSS, normal podcast apps"
- "Summaries first, audio later"
- "Your reading becomes a wiki"

## Capture Safety Checklist

Before exporting any video:

- Use demo data only.
- Blur or crop all private feed URLs, feed tokens, media signatures, emails, session details, Stripe IDs, and share signatures.
- Avoid real private article titles unless they are public and intentionally shown.
- Use placeholder item titles for demos, for example "AI agents field guide", "Market memo", "Research PDF", "Architecture essay".
- Keep app UI readable on a phone at 50% brightness.
- Watch the first 2 seconds without sound; the point should still be clear.

## Review Gate

Before publishing each short, confirm:

- First frame has a clear promise.
- Spoken hook starts immediately.
- Product proof appears by 10 seconds.
- Captions are correct for `ArticlePod`, `RSS`, `TTS`, and `Summary Mode`.
- No private token, email, Stripe ID, share signature, or real private content is visible.
- Video is understandable without sound for the first 2 seconds.
- CTA is specific: try one article, comment with workflow, reply for access, or ask a question.
